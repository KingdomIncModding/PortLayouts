[b]Port Rotation[/b]
Choose which sides of your Kingdom Inc buildings hold their inputs and outputs. The building body and icon stay upright, with ports, pipe connections and snapping following the selected layout.

[b]Unlock and controls[/b]
Open [b]Tech Tree > Mods[/b] and research [b]Port Rotation[/b] for free.
[list]
[*][b]Shift + Alt + left-click[/b] a building or its label to cycle: Left/Right, Top/Bottom, Left/Top, Left/Bottom, Right/Top, Right/Bottom. Inputs are listed first.[/*]
[*][b]Alt + left-click[/b] reverses the current pair. Ordinary flipping remains available before research.[/*]
[/list]
Cycling returns to Left/Right after the sixth layout and resets reversal to the listed direction.

[b]Labels and connections[/b]
[list]
[*]Labels overlap a border without ports, matching normal upright labels.[/*]
[*]Top and bottom labels read normally. Side labels run sideways with the bottoms of the letters facing the building.[/*]
[*]Labels hide during dragging and appear when hovering over a building or its ports. The building being moved keeps its own label.[/*]
[*]Existing pipe connections and manual bends remain intact. Port artwork, connection points and placement previews stay aligned.[/*]
[*]Layouts, reversal and research progress save with the kingdom. Native copying preserves layouts.[/*]
[*]Existing rotated buildings retain their layout after updating, even before the new research is purchased.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Expandable buildings[/b]
Buildings with expandable port slots keep their native Left/Right ports. Shift+Alt does nothing on them; Alt still flips them normally.

[b]Installation and updating[/b]
Use the Mono edition of Kingdom Inc with BepInEx 5. Install the requirements listed on this page, close the game and extract the player ZIP into the game folder.

The plugin must be at [b]BepInEx/plugins/PortLayouts/PortLayouts.dll[/b]. Replace the existing DLL when updating and restart the game. The internal name remains [b]Port Layouts[/b].

[b]Removal[/b]
Close the game and remove only PortLayouts.dll. Buildings return to native Left/Right ports with their normal mirrored setting retained. Saving without the plugin drops custom layout information; keep a save backup if you intend to restore it later.
