Port Rotation 1.0.12
By Urakka (rockyamyx)

Choose which sides of a building hold its inputs and outputs. The building and its icon stay upright.

INSTALLATION
Use Kingdom Inc's Mono edition with BepInEx 5. Install the requirements listed on the download page.
Close the game and extract this ZIP into the game directory.
The plugin must be at BepInEx/plugins/PortLayouts/PortLayouts.dll.
When updating, replace the existing DLL and restart the game. Its internal name remains Port Layouts.

UNLOCK AND CONTROLS
Open Tech Tree > Mods and research Port Rotation for free.
Shift + Alt + left-click a building or its label to cycle input/output layouts:
Left/Right, Top/Bottom, Left/Top, Left/Bottom, Right/Top, Right/Bottom.
Alt + left-click reverses the selected pair. Ordinary flipping works before research.
Selecting the next layout resets reversal. Expandable-slot buildings keep native Left/Right ports.

LABELS AND CONNECTIONS
Labels overlap a border without ports. Top/bottom text reads normally; side text faces the building.
Labels hide during dragging and reappear when hovering over the building or its ports.
The building being moved keeps its own label visible.
Existing pipe connections and manual bends are retained. Ports, snapping and copy previews stay aligned.
Layouts, reversal and research progress save with the kingdom. Native copying retains layouts.
Existing rotated buildings retain their layouts after updating, even before the new research is purchased.

REMOVAL
Close the game and remove BepInEx/plugins/PortLayouts/PortLayouts.dll.
Buildings return to native Left/Right ports with their normal mirrored setting retained.
Saving without the plugin drops custom layout information; keep a save backup to restore it later.
